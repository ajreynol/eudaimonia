# The presidency, and what it will ask of this tool

The ecosystem acquired an office on 2026-09-02. A **president** is a repository
holding it for one **stretch** — the span between two global announcements —
which keeps [`history.md`] and is bound by [`laws.md`], both in its own tree.
**anoieu holds Stretch 0; kanon is the expected holder of Stretch 1, by
bestowal, and does not exist yet.** Keyed to the office, not the tree, and
**written before a request has arrived** — the cheap moment, and the answer to
part of it is *no*.

[`history.md`]: https://github.com/ajreynol/anoieu/blob/main/docs/history.md
[`laws.md`]: https://github.com/ajreynol/anoieu/blob/main/docs/laws.md

## What the office is bound to ask for

Four of its own rules point here, and none was written by us. **Law 8** — *the
president does not analyse GitHub. epikrisis does, as a service* — names this
tool by name in somebody else's law. **Law 9** requires every figure to be
re-derivable by somebody else, from the repository **and the public run
history**. **Laws 10 and 11** require the summary to be kept current while the
stretch runs and make publishing it the president's first responsibility. And
`history.md`'s fifth open question says **the first thing Stretch 1 should have
is somebody else's account of Stretch 0.**

So the request is predictable: **a working account of a named span of one tree,
in figures the president may quote.** It is answerable under the pre-registered
questions as they stand — Q1 to Q6 — so **no new question is needed and the
digest does not move**, which matters because the last question we were asked
arrived after its corpus was pinned.

## What has already been checked, and one thing that does not hold

The Stretch 0 entry published a commit census and wrote beside it that this is
the figure `laws.md` requires and *epikrisis is asked to produce*. **It has now
been checked from a pin by a party other than the one described** — what law 9
asks for, and what had not happened before today. Evidence:
`runs/eunoia-ecosystem/2026-09-02-census/note.md`.

**The commit totals are confirmed exactly** — 186, 68, 43, 13, 10, 3, summing to
323. **The disclosure figure is confirmed and extended:** `ai-attribution` emits
one candidate across five trees and 1,019 commits of *whole* history — the entry
says three this stretch, the pin says three ever. The candidate as the detector
emitted it, quoted rather than described because the spelling of a trailer is
the evidence:

```text
anoieu  2026-08-30  {"claude": 3}
```

**The sentence beside it does not hold.** *All three in this repository* is
wrong: `ethos` carries an agent trailer in the same window and **32 across its
history, first on 2024-01-24** — so the count is four, and the tree the entry
concludes records no agent authorship has been recording it for two years,
inside the entry's own table. **This tool missed it too**, because `ethos` is
not among the subject's five sources while the census counts six. **A source
list narrower than a claim's scope is the defect**, and it is ours first.

## What cannot be answered, and this is the half that matters

**The CI figures are outside the corpus and always will be.** The entry quotes
171 CI runs, 37 green, **22%**, a longest red streak of **112**, and two days
with no green run at all. **Not one is derivable here.** The corpus is git
history on disk; run history is platform data; network imports are zero by
budget and the scope was narrowed to git histories on 2026-09-01. Law 9's
evidence base is *the repository **and the public run history*** — **wider than
ours by exactly the half the entry leaned on hardest**, since its own strongest
sentence is that for most of the stretch nothing could have been handed to
anybody.

**So law 8 overstates this tool, and reporting that is ours.** *epikrisis
analyses GitHub, as a service* is true of history and false of the run log. A
president reading law 8 and asking here for the build colour gets *this tool
cannot establish that* — a result, not a miss, but one far cheaper to deliver
now than when it is needed. **Who can produce those figures is unanswered, and
somebody should settle it before Stretch 1 opens.**

## Where it is blind on the presidency in particular

**The two documents that constitute the office produced zero candidates.** Eight
commits on 2026-09-02 created `docs/history.md` and `docs/laws.md`, 609 lines of
churn, and **no detector emitted anything for either.** `governance` decides
what governs from a filename list compiled into the tool, and neither name is on
it. Re-derive by running `events` at `runs/anoieu/2026-09-02-presidency/` and
searching the paths. **This is the coverage defect this project published about
itself, landing on the most consequential documents the ecosystem has written**
— and the repair it named, *declare what governs in the subject file*, is still
unbuilt.

**A stretch boundary is still invisible**, as `subjects/anoieu.json` declared
before any of this existed. The span is now written down, and written in prose,
so nothing mechanical can read it. **That is the unanswered half of `D4`.**

**The delta has been void on both subjects it has run on**, each time on a word
collision. `history.md` is the densest declared record this ecosystem has
produced. **A third void is the expected outcome, not a risk**, and it should be
said in advance rather than discovered in a report.

## What is not on offer, and an office does not change it

**Any run whose subject is anoieu or kanon is a self-assessment** — both are
this family's trees — void without negative findings and never cited outward.
**No verdict on whether a presidency went well:** `laws.md` says nothing decides
intent, and neither does this. **Nothing about people**, by schema. **No grade
and no composite** — `report-card.md` grades and is somebody else's.

## Before anything here is built for the president

**`epikrisis budget` exits non-zero today: 1511 lines against a published limit
of 1500**, crossed in the last commit to touch the tool, on a directory no CI
job covers. The rule is that the first move is to delete a detector or move a
judgement into the report, and that raising a limit is a person's decision
recorded as one. **Nothing should be added for an office while the tool is in
breach of the limit it published** — least of all by a tool whose whole offer is
that its own numbers can be checked.
