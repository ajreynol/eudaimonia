# Documentation

Every document in this child project, and what each one is for. The front page,
[`../README.md`](../README.md), is the entry point, and both documents here
assume it has been read.

| document | what it is for |
| --- | --- |
| [`analogy.md`](analogy.md) | The Eudaimonia build system and this one, stage by stage: what transfers, where it breaks, and why the boundary at repository creation is placed where it is. Maintained as the thing this project is for. |
| [`ai-workflows.md`](ai-workflows.md) | The findings register: what this ecosystem is doing that looks novel and might generalise, and what is wrong with it, on one list. Each entry carries its evidence and what would falsify it. |
| [`boundary.md`](boundary.md) | What analysis here can establish, what it can establish only through somebody, and what it cannot establish at all — the three zones, the six reasons a question sits in the middle one, and the decision procedure. |

Three documents, and the third had to buy its way in. The rule was two, and it
was deliberate: the ecosystem's own account of this failure is blunt — a stretch
of work that produced thousands of lines of governance documentation changed
nothing about what any tool found, and writing one more page is the comfortable
alternative to doing the work. A further document here has to displace a
question in [`../interview.md`](../interview.md) or an hour of somebody's
reading.

[`boundary.md`](boundary.md) claims the second, and states the claim so it can
be refused: the line it draws is drawn in dozens of places across five
repositories, and where two statements of it have been laid side by side they
disagree fourteen times — so knowing where it falls today means reading all of
them and reconciling them by hand. If somebody checks that and finds three
disagreements rather than fourteen, the document has not earned its slot and
goes. **A fourth displaces one of these three.**
